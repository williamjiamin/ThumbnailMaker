# ThumbnailMaker Competitor Research Matrix

Last updated: 2026-06-29

External source spot-check: 2026-06-29 against the official URLs listed in the Sources section.

Purpose: define what ThumbnailMaker must match, avoid, and beat for a credible creator-thumbnail beta and longer-term "best niche thumbnail app" roadmap.

## ThumbnailMaker Current Baseline

Static repo inspection plus the beta-exit evidence log now show these foundations. Important caveat: live-repo Xcode commands are still blocked by the local `xcodebuild -list` hang recorded in `docs/BETA_EXIT_CHECKLIST.md`, so the strongest build/test/screenshot proof currently comes from isolated current-working-tree verifier copies, not from a clean release checkout or Mac mini CI.

- Export presets exist for YouTube thumbnail, Shorts/TikTok/Reels, Instagram square, Instagram Reels cover, podcast/cover art, general video cover, portrait cover, and custom size paths in `ThumbnailMaker/Models/ThumbnailExportModel.swift`.
- Export format model includes PNG, JPEG, and WebP, with compression-quality support for lossy formats and runtime platform encode checks.
- Built-in templates exist, but the library is still very small: five starter templates across YouTube, short-form, podcast, gaming, and vlog in `ThumbnailMaker/Models/ThumbnailTemplate.swift`.
- Local suggestion foundations exist: text shortening, template suggestions, brand color suggestions, face-crop suggestions, and local title/layout idea generation in `ThumbnailMaker/Models/ThumbnailSuggestion.swift` and `ThumbnailMaker/Models/ThumbnailIdea.swift`.
- Privacy-first subject processing exists in `ThumbnailMaker/Services/BackgroundRemovalService.swift` using Vision/Core Image foreground masks and face detection where available.
- Brand kit foundations exist in currently untracked/in-flight files: `ThumbnailMaker/Models/BrandKit.swift`, `ThumbnailMaker/Services/BrandKitStore.swift`, and `ThumbnailMaker/Views/Components/BrandKitEditorView.swift`.
- WYSIWYG layer/canvas beta scope now has isolated current-working-tree UI proof for text, profile, image, and shape layers, plus layer-system proof for text/image/shape/sticker-ready content layers, reorder, duplicate/delete, snapping, and accessibility. Mac mini CI and clean release-branch proof are still missing.
- No web app exists in this repo yet.

## Source Notes

- YouTube Help currently recommends 3840 x 2160 thumbnails with minimum width 640, JPG/GIF/PNG, 16:9, and device-dependent file-size limits. Several thumbnail-maker pages still describe 1280 x 720; treat 3840 x 2160 as the high-resolution YouTube export target and consider 1280 x 720 as a smaller optional export preset.
- Instagram Help pages can require login or return inconsistent public access. Keep Instagram/Reels size guidance marked for final verification before App Store copy or hard-coded claims.

## Better-Than-Competitor Roadmap

1. Creator-specific quality gates: small-preview readability, contrast, safe-zone, upload limit, aspect-ratio, and face visibility checks.
2. Privacy-first cutout flow: on-device subject mask, on-device face crop, clear unavailable-state messaging, and no cloud upload without explicit consent.
3. Prompt-to-edit architecture: AI returns editable document/layer mutations, not flattened image-only results.
4. Variant packs: generate three title/layout/color variants and export them for YouTube A/B tests or creator review.
5. Platform-safe previews: show YouTube watch, YouTube Shorts frame caveat, TikTok profile/search, Instagram Reels cover crop, Instagram square, and podcast square previews.
6. Niche templates: gaming, reaction, education, finance, fitness, podcast, product review, tutorial, vlog, shorts hook, and news/commentary.
7. Apple-native advantage: fast local drafts, share sheet, Photos/Files export, keyboard/pointer support on iPad/Mac, and App Store privacy clarity.

## Immediate Product Decisions

- Keep the beta promise narrow: iOS, real iPad, and Catalyst first. Web remains a roadmap item until the document schema and renderer are stable.
- Keep AI claims honest: "local title/layout suggestions" and "on-device cutout where supported" are beta-safe; "AI-generated thumbnails" requires more implementation and privacy disclosure.
- Do not ship unfinished AI/template/brand-kit surfaces without either test coverage or feature gating.
- Treat YouTube Studio requirements as authoritative for YouTube export: 3840x2160, 16:9, JPG/GIF/PNG, device-specific file-size limits, podcast square, and Shorts caveats.
- Treat TikTok and Instagram as cover-preview workflows, not just dimensions; creators need crop/safe-zone previews before export.
