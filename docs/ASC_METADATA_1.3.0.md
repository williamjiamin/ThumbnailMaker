## Description (≤4000)
Make scroll-stopping thumbnails and video covers in minutes — right on your iPhone, iPad or Mac.

Start with Quick Create: pick a format, choose a template, type your title and export. Or jump into the full editor and build any design layer by layer.

MADE FOR CREATORS
• Formats for YouTube (16:9), Shorts, TikTok & Reels (9:16), Instagram (1:1), Facebook (4:3) and Pinterest (3:4)
• A small-size preview so you can check your thumbnail the way viewers actually see it in the feed
• Safe-zone guides and readability checks for text size, length and contrast

TEST YOUR PACKAGING
• Make up to three variants (A, B, C) of one design, each with its own undo history
• Compare them side by side at real feed size and mark your pick
• Export every variant in every size in one go, with clear file names ready for YouTube's Test & Compare

GRAB THE PERFECT FRAME
• Pull a frame straight from your video and get suggestions for the sharpest, most expressive moments — all on your device

DESIGN TOOLS
• Text with outlines, shadows, glow, background panels, spacing and auto-fit
• Arrows, badges, callouts, stickers, emoji and freehand drawing
• Solid and gradient backgrounds
• Non-destructive crop, fit and fill — your original photo is never altered
• One-tap background removal, with brushes to refine the cutout
• Reversible photo adjustments and saved looks
• Multi-select, group, align and distribute layers
• 20 fonts, including Chinese, Japanese and Korean

WORK FASTER
• A project library with search, duplicate and recoverable trash
• Save your own templates and multiple brand kits
• Resize one design into landscape, square and vertical versions
• Make a whole series from a list of titles (or a CSV) in one batch
• Export and import editable project files

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your photos and designs stay on your device. No account, no tracking.
