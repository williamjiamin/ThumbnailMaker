Simple Thumbnail Maker Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Overview

Simple Thumbnail Maker is developed by LIPAI WANG for iOS and Android. It does not require an account. The developer does not operate a server that receives your projects or imported media.

Projects and imported media

Projects, preferences, and media you choose through the platform's picker are processed and stored locally. The App does not upload your project files or imported media to a developer server. Local data may be included in backups controlled by you and your platform provider.

YouTube input

If you enter a YouTube URL, the App sends the extracted public video identifier to Google's YouTube service to retrieve public video metadata or thumbnail imagery. Google's handling of that request is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Advertising on Android

The Android version displays ads through Google Mobile Ads. Google's SDK may collect or share IP address for approximate location, app interactions, diagnostics, and device or account identifiers for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention.

The Android version uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for consent where required. When Google requires a persistent privacy-options entry point, open Settings and select Privacy choices to review or change the available advertising choices.

Purchases

Optional purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store on iOS and by Google through Google Play on Android. The developer does not receive your payment-card details.

Deleting data

Clear App storage or delete the App to remove local data, subject to backups managed by you and your platform provider.
