Privacy Policy for Simple Thumbnail Maker

Last Updated: December 10, 2024

1. Information We Access
1.1. Photo Library Access
- We request access to your device's photo library
- This access is used solely to:
  * Save created thumbnails
  * Select images for thumbnail creation
- We do not upload or store your images on any servers

1.2. YouTube Integration
- When using the YouTube import feature, we:
  * Access YouTube Data API to fetch video information
  * Download video thumbnails directly to your device
  * Track daily import quota usage locally on your device
- We do not store YouTube video data on our servers
- Video information is processed entirely on your device

2. How We Use Information
2.1. Photo Library
- Only accessed when you explicitly choose to:
  * Save a thumbnail
  * Select an image for editing
- All image processing is done locally on your device

2.2. YouTube Access
- We use the YouTube Data API solely to fetch publicly available:
  * Video thumbnails
  * Video titles
- This information is only accessed when you explicitly use the YouTube import feature
- We maintain a local counter for your daily import quota

2.3. Purchase Information
- Premium feature purchase status is stored locally
- Purchase records are managed by Apple's App Store

3. Data Collection
3.1. We Do Not Collect:
- Personal information
- Usage statistics
- Images or thumbnails
- Location data
- Device information
- YouTube browsing habits

3.2. Locally Stored Information:
- YouTube import quota usage
- Last import reset date
- Premium purchase status

3.3. App Store Data
- Standard App Store analytics may be collected by Apple

4. Data Storage
- All app data is stored locally on your device
- No cloud storage or external servers are used
- Your images remain on your device
- YouTube thumbnails are saved only to your device

5. Third-Party Services
- Apple App Store for app distribution and purchases
- YouTube Data API for fetching public video information
- No other third-party services are used

6. Children's Privacy
- Our app does not collect information from children
- No age-restricted features

7. Your Rights
You have the right to:
- Control photo library access through device settings
- Request premium purchase refunds through Apple
- Delete the app and all local data

8. Changes to Privacy Policy
- We will notify users of any privacy policy changes
- Continued use constitutes acceptance of changes
